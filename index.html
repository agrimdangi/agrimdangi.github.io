Employee Performance &
Workforce Intelligence

An integrated HR analytics platform designed to turn workforce data into actionable insights. It combines interactive Power BI reporting, an employee search portal and locally generated AI recommendations.

3,000SYNTHETIC EMPLOYEE RECORDS
10DEPARTMENTS
2024–25DATA PERIOD

What it does

  • Explores performance, attendance, compensation and engagement metrics.
  • Provides employee-level search and workforce profiles through Flask.
  • Highlights attrition-risk indicators to support HR review.
  • Uses Ollama for explainable recommendations, training suggestions and 30-day improvement plans.
