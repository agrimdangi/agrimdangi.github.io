Building an Employee Performance & Workforce Intelligence Dashboard

Turning workforce data into useful views for HR teams with Power BI, Python and Flask.

Human resource teams work with many kinds of information: employee records, attendance, performance reviews, compensation, training and engagement surveys. When these datasets are difficult to explore together, it becomes harder to spot patterns and ask useful follow-up questions.

The project idea

I built an academic demonstration platform that brings several HR analytics workflows into one place. The goal is to make workforce indicators easier to explore, not to automate decisions about people.

Data and analytics

The project uses a synthetic dataset representing 3,000 employees across 10 departments, covering January 2024 through December 2025. The data is organized into employee and department dimensions alongside attendance, compensation, performance, survey and training facts.

Power Query supports data cleaning and transformation, while DAX measures support dashboard KPIs and analysis. The dashboard explores workforce size, attendance, performance, engagement, compensation and attrition-related indicators.

The application layer

A Flask application provides an employee search experience using employee IDs such as E00001 or an employee name. The intent is to make an individual profile easier to inspect alongside organization-level reporting.

Responsible interpretation

Attrition-risk indicators are signals for further review, not conclusions about an individual. The data is synthetic, and any real-world deployment would require strong privacy controls, validation, fairness evaluation and appropriate HR governance.

What I learned

  • Good dashboards begin with clear business questions and metric definitions.
  • Data quality and consistent date formats matter across tools.
  • Employee-level context should complement, not replace, aggregate analysis.
  • Documentation is essential for explaining how metrics are calculated.

The project is an ongoing learning exercise in combining business intelligence, web development and responsible AI.
